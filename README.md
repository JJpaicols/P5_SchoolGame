# 🎮 [DANTE'S JOURNEY]

"Nothing to see here, just a quirky game project built with p5.js."

## 🚀 The Mission
You are a brave pixel-soul trapped in a platformer world. Your only goal? Reach the **Key** placed at the top of the map. The catch? Gravity is your enemy, platforms are your friends, and those moving creatures? They are definitely not there to give you a hug—touch them, and it's Game Over.

## 🛠 Tech Stack & Nerd Stuff
This project was a fun dive into creative coding for my school curriculum. Under the hood, you'll find:
* **Language:** JavaScript (because the web runs on chaos and JS).
* **Library:** [p5.js](https://p5js.org/) (making canvas manipulation slightly less painful).
* **Paradigm:** Object-Oriented Programming (yes, I actually treated my enemies and platforms like objects, fancy right?).

## 🕹 How to Play
* **Move:** [Arrow keys/WASD]
* **Jump:** [Spacebar]
* **Goal:** Reach the shiny Key at the top before you lose your mind.

## ⚠️ Known Issues (or "Features")
* If you find a bug, it’s probably a hidden feature. 
* If the physics feel weird, blame the engine (or my implementation, let's keep it 50/50).
